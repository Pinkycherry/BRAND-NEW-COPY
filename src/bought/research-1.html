        How do you prepare data for an AI market research report?

        Gathering reliable inputs prevents AI engines from hallucinating TAM figures or misidentifying indirect competitors as primary threats. Founders must supply precise constraints including target geography, initial customer ARPU, and core product differentiation before generating a report. Combining internal CRM data with external vertical tags forces the model to synthesize specific unit economics rather than relying on generic macro estimates. To maximize report accuracy, structure your prompt with explicitly bounded parameters, negative constraints, and verified baseline metrics.

        • Define your exact ICP including firmographic size, annual budget, and tech stack requirements.
        • Provide a baseline target pricing range to anchor all bottom-up TAM and SAM calculations.
        • List known direct competitors to force the engine into deep-tail niche identification.

        What are common mistakes when using AI for market research?

        The most frequent failure in automated market research is trusting top-down market sizing without performing a bottom-up sanity check. AI models frequently scrape outdated press releases that overestimate total addressable market by conflating broad enterprise software spending with specific niche software budgets. Another common pitfall is failing to verify competitor pricing models, as web scrapers often miss custom enterprise Tiers or annual discount structures. Founders must manually cross-reference AI-generated pricing lists against current sales call feedback.

        • Top-down TAM inflation occurs when AI scrapes generic industry reports instead of niche data.
        • Unverified competitor pricing often misses hidden enterprise tiers and custom implementation fees.
        • Over-reliance on outdated web citations leads to missing newly funded stealth startups.
