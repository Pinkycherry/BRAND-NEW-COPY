Standing rules for any agent continuing this work

  1. One page at a time: commit, push to main, reply with only the live URL. Wait for the founder before the next page.
  2. Seller layout exactly: same sections, order, components, CSS and images (for now). Our header and footer unchanged. Never load the seller router script; rebuild tabs, accordions, scroll reveals and hovers in our own small script.
  3. Slugs: the full phrase an Indian types into Google or asks Gemini (main keyword + niche + India). Slug, SEO title, H1 and meta are ours, never the seller’s with a word added. No redirects while the site is noindex; update every internal link instead.
  4. Noindex: the whole site stays noindex until the founder says otherwise.
  5. Logs: only BBI Site Copy Log (one entry per push) and India Topic Pages Review (this tracker). Rules also saved in the repo CLAUDE.md under “Founder rules — India topic pages”.
  6. E-E-A-T on every page: named reviewer or author line linking to /about, a last-updated date, a sources section with real Indian sources (RBI, MSME, Startup India, government portals), and first-hand practical steps.
  7. Q&A pages: no new Q&A research. Use our FAQ page and Supabase FAQ data inside the seller question-page structure.
  8. Internal links: link every page richly to the 10 topic pages, lists, categories, ideas, calculators, guides and glossary, in related sections and inline. Add links back from older pages.
  9. Images: every guide page gets its 16:9 guide image, every blog or article page its featured image; seller images for now.
  10. Content: Indian context, warm “you” voice, no invented numbers, laws or sources; future-proof wording that is not anchored to old years. No seller brand, no seller links, no href="#".
  11. Quality: every seller section in full long-form writing; real Indian portal steps; worked rupee examples; metro, tier 2, tier 3 and village differences; sector differences; Indian mistakes and family and cash realities; checklists, worksheets and a this-week plan; rich inline links. No researched figures (rule of 5 Nov 2026): where a number is needed, the page shows a Validate line pointing to its Validate button. No new pages until all existing pages are rewritten.
  12. Push route: git push is refused in this sandbox, so commits go through the GitHub web upload page in the founder's browser, one folder per upload. New folders cannot be created that way; data files live in src/data/.
